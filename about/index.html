About

I finished my Master's in Computer Science at UT Dallas in December 2025. Alongside it I interned at ISN on multi-agent systems and a multimodal risk advisor, and I still do research at UT Dallas on adversarial red-teaming agents for network protocols.

On the side I contribute to OpenClimateFix, an open-source group forecasting solar power, and I run my own GPU server at home for local models.
